Demo photo source notes

The venue and trainer profiles are fictional. The three local portrait WebPs were generated for this demo. The remaining images are remote Unsplash photos used to show visual direction.

Portrait brief: natural head-and-shoulders gym portraits, plain training shirts, soft side light and a dark gym background.
- maja-portrait.webp: Maja, strength and mobility coach by a squat rack, tied-back brown hair.
- luka-portrait.webp: Luka, HIIT coach by kettlebells, short brown hair and light stubble.
- nina-portrait.webp: Nina, personal trainer by dumbbells, swept-back dark blonde hair.
